# Weathero Design System (MASTER)

## Foundations
- **Background (OLED):** #0a0a0a
- **Accent (sky):** #38bdf8
- **Typography:** Plus Jakarta Sans — 400 (body), 600 (headings)

## Glass (selective)
- Use glass surfaces sparingly on cards, modals, and overlays only.
- --glass-bg: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)
- --glass-border: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)
- --glass-blur: 12px

## Principles
- Ambient dark OLED dashboard; minimal chrome; high contrast text on #0a0a0a.
